# Study Notes: A Grand Unification of Quantum Algorithms

## Overview
This repository contains comprehensive study notes based on the seminal paper "A Grand Unification of Quantum Algorithms" by Martyn et al. (2021).

Rather than a simple summary, these notes represent a structural reconstruction of the paper's core concepts. The goal is to digest the complex mathematical framework of Quantum Singular Value Transformation (QSVT) and interpret it based on my current understanding.

- Original Paper: [A Grand Unification of Quantum Algorithms (arXiv:2105.02859)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02859)
- Author of Notes: Ryo Fujita (Kyushu University)

## Key Objectives of the Notes
I have reorganized the paper's content to make the flow of algorithm construction more intuitive for readers. The notes focus on:

1.  Logical Bridging: Supplementing the logical leaps in the original paper to clarify the transition from individually proposed quantum algorithms to the generalized QSVT.
2.  Visualizing the Workflow: Reconstructing the derivation steps to help readers easily visualize how abstract operators are transformed into concrete algorithms.
3.  Focus on "The Big Picture": While detailed approximation theories (e.g., specific coefficients of Remez algorithm) are treated as given modules, the notes place maximum emphasis on understanding the architecture of QSVT itself-how to embed, transform, and extract quantum information.
